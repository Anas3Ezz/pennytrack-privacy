Penny Track

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026 · Last updated: October 16, 2026

Short version: Penny Track works entirely offline. All of your information stays on your device, encrypted. We do not collect, receive, store, transmit, sell, or share any of your data — we have no servers and no access to it.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Penny Track mobile application ("Penny Track", "the app", "we", "us") handles your information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Penny Track does not require an account, sign-in, or any personal details to use.

The app stores the information you choose to enter so it can show it back to you — for example:

  • Transactions you add (title, amount, type, category, date, and optional note)
  • Budgets you set (category and limit)
  • App preferences (such as theme and selected currency)

This information is saved only in local storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

2. How your data is stored and protected

  • Your financial data is stored only on your device.
  • It is encrypted at rest using AES encryption.
  • The encryption key is held in your device's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) and never leaves your device.

3. Data sharing and third parties

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose your data to anyone. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no trackers, and no third-party data-collection SDKs. No data is transmitted off your device.

4. Permissions and network access

Penny Track does not require internet access to function and does not request sensitive permissions in order to collect data. The app operates fully offline.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, you are in full control of it:

  • Delete individual transactions or budgets from within the app at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its locally stored data from your device.

6. Children's privacy

Penny Track is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children (we do not collect personal information from anyone).

7. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change in the future (for example, if an optional cloud-sync or crash-reporting feature is added), we will update this policy and revise the "Last updated" date above before that feature is released.
