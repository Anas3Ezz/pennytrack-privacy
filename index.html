Penny Track

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Short version: Penny Track works offline. All of your financial information stays on your device, encrypted. We do not collect, receive, store, transmit, sell, or share any of your data — we have no servers and no access to it. Optional Pro purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Penny Track mobile application ("Penny Track", "the app", "we", "us") handles your information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Penny Track does not require an account, sign-in, or any personal details to use.

The app stores the information you choose to enter so it can show it back to you — for example:

  • Transactions you add (title, amount, type, category, date, and optional note)
  • Budgets you set (category and limit)
  • App preferences (such as theme, language, currency, and reminder settings)
  • Whether you have unlocked Penny Track Pro

This information is saved only in local storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

2. How your data is stored and protected

  • Your financial data is stored only on your device.
  • It is encrypted at rest using AES encryption.
  • The encryption key is held in your device's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) and never leaves your device.

3. Messages you share with the app

You can choose to share a bank or wallet message (for example, a transfer confirmation) with Penny Track using your device's Share menu. When you do:

  • The app reads that text on your device only to pre-fill a new transaction (amount, income or expense, and source).
  • The original message text is not saved and is never sent anywhere. Only the transaction you review and confirm is stored.
  • Penny Track does not read your SMS messages or notifications and cannot see anything you do not explicitly share with it.

4. Notifications and home-screen widget

  • Budget alerts and expense reminders are local notifications created and scheduled on your device. They are not sent from any server.
  • The optional home-screen widget shows your remaining budget. The summary it displays is kept in storage on your device that only the app and its widget can read.

5. In-app purchases

Penny Track Pro is an optional one-time purchase. Payment is processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. We never receive your payment details. The app only receives confirmation that Pro is unlocked and stores that on your device so it can be restored.

6. Backups and exports

You can choose to export your transactions as a CSV spreadsheet or create a backup file of your transactions, budgets, and currency. When you do:

  • The file is created only when you ask, and it is sent only to the place you pick in your device's Share menu (for example, your own cloud storage, email, or files).
  • Backup and CSV files are not encrypted. Once you share a file, it is handled by the app or service you sent it to, so keep it somewhere safe.
  • When you restore, the app reads the file you choose on your device only. We never receive or see your backup files.

7. Data sharing and third parties

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose your data to anyone. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, no trackers, and no third-party data-collection SDKs. Your financial data is never transmitted off your device.

8. Permissions and network access

Penny Track's features work fully offline. The only network communication is between your device and the app store when you buy or restore Pro. The app requests these permissions:

  • Notifications — to show the budget alerts and reminders you turn on.
  • Run at startup — to reschedule your reminders after the device restarts.

The app does not request access to your SMS, contacts, location, camera, or microphone. It only opens a file when you pick one yourself to restore a backup.

9. Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, you are in full control of it:

  • Delete individual transactions or budgets from within the app at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its locally stored data from your device. To keep your data, create a backup file first and restore it after reinstalling.

10. Children's privacy

Penny Track is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children (we do not collect personal information from anyone).

11. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change in the future (for example, if an optional cloud-sync or crash-reporting feature is added), we will update this policy and revise the "Last updated" date above before that feature is released.
